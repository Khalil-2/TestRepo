## 2026-05-30 - [Real-time Input & Async Feedback]
**Learning:** Providing real-time character counts and simulated loading states significantly improves perceived responsiveness and accessibility. Using aria-live for both counter updates and success messages ensures screen reader users are kept in the loop without losing context.
**Action:** Always pair character-limited inputs with live counters and ensure async actions provide clear visual and programmatic "loading" and "done" states.
