## 2026-03-13 - [README as the Primary Interface]
**Learning:** In a minimal or empty repository, the `README.md` serves as the primary "User Interface" for developers. A lack of structure, context, or accessibility considerations in the README creates a poor onboarding experience and high cognitive load.
**Action:** When encountering an empty repository, prioritize the `README.md` as a UX surface. Ensure it follows a clear structure, includes an accessibility statement, and provides immediate value through a "Getting Started" guide.
