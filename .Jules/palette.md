## 2025-03-25 - [Label in Name (WCAG 2.5.3)]
**Learning:** For accessibility and voice-control users, the `aria-label` must contain the visible text of the element (e.g., a button or link) to ensure a consistent experience and avoid confusion when the accessible name differs from the visual label.
**Action:** Always include the visible text in any `aria-label` applied to an element, even if adding additional descriptive context.
